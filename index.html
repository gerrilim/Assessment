Social Style Self-Assessment

This assessment is based on the Social Styles, which is a model for understanding people’s behavioural styles. Everyone has natural behaviours and preferred communication styles. The purpose of this self-assessment is to help you identify your personal style preferences and learn how to adapt your style to improve interpersonal relationships.

Confidentiality
Your results will be confidential, only Talent Incubator will have access to individual responses which will not be shared. Responses will be aggregated for a group-level report.

Instructions
Answer all 24 questions using the scale: Mostly Left | Slightly Left | Slightly Right | Mostly Right.
There are no right or wrong answers – respond based on what you do, rather than what should be.

Please provide your name and email so that the results can be submitted and also sent to you.

Your results will be sent to geraldine.lim@talentincubator.net.
